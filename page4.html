Page 4: Large Herbivores (Grid Layout)

1. Reindeer / Deer

Reindeer, also known as caribou in North America, are hoofed mammals adapted to cold arctic and subarctic climates. Both male and female reindeer grow impressive antlers, and their hooves adapt seasonally to trek across soft tundra snow or icy ground.

Learn more on Wikipedia.

2. Camel

Camels are remarkable desert mammals known for storing fatty tissue in their humps, which converts into water and energy when resources are scarce. They have specialized nostrils, thick eyelashes, and cushioned pads on their feet suited for harsh desert sands.

Learn more on Wikipedia.
