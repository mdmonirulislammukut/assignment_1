Page 2: Reptiles & Aquatic Life

1. Giant Tortoise

Giant tortoises are among the longest-living animals on Earth, often living well over a century. Characterized by their massive heavy shells and slow movements, they are native to island habitats such as the Galápagos and Aldabra, where they play a vital ecological role as herbivores.

Learn more about tortoises on Wikipedia.

2. Koi Fish

Koi are decorative varieties of domesticated common carp that are kept in outdoor ponds and water gardens for their striking colors and patterns. Symbolizing good fortune, perseverance, and courage in Asian cultures, koi can live for several decades in well-maintained environments.

Learn more about koi on Wikipedia.
